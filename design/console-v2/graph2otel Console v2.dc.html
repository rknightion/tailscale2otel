2c

The family small-chart standard

Defined here, inherited by all four consoles. Series read by stroke pattern and label, never hue alone.
Anatomy, single series
heap 78M
44px left gutter for three axis labels at 9px mono. Three gridlines at min, mid, max in --color-border-default. Area fill at 10 percent, single series only. Hover draws a dashed crosshair and a 6px-radius tooltip on the raised surface.
Three series, no area
series 1 · petrol · solid series 2 · ink · dashed series 3 · ochre · dotted
Three is the ceiling. A fourth series means the chart is answering two questions and should be two charts. Series order is fixed, so the same measure keeps the same stroke on every console.
Fewer than two points
collecting, 1 of 2 samples
A single point is not a chart. Say so; never draw a dot and imply a trend.
Nothing to chart
self-observability off
A switched-off source draws nothing, and states the switch. Not a flat line at zero.
Sparkline, in a table cell
2 of 12 failed
100x22, no axis, no area, 1.5px non-scaling stroke. The outcome strip beside it carries pass and fail as full-height cells plus a count.
